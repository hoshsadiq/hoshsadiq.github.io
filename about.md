# Hey, I'm Hosh!

I'm a platform and systems engineer. Most of my work is making complex systems understandable, operable, secure, and, ideally, boring.

I design and run cloud platforms, networks, and automation systems, often in regulated or high-stakes environments. I think a lot about first principles, explicit constraints, and how systems fail, not just how they work when everything is green. I like making it harder for things to go wrong quietly. I build better defaults and guardrails, without relying on careful humans following a runbook.

This blog is mostly my technical notes, and whatever doesn't fit elsewhere.

If you are interested in infrastructure, distributed systems, cloud platforms, or security, I hope I can show you the typical Kurdish hospitality and make you feel at home.
